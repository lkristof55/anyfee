anyfee

Solana vaults for GitHub and X accounts · devnet

A vault is unverified until claimed and is not an endorsement by the account it belongs to; the recipient may decline. Direct tips return to their senders after 30 days unclaimed. Creator fees have no single sender and wait for the owner. The attester can bind a vault, never withdraw from one. anyfee has no token. Devnet only: no real funds, unaudited, open source (Apache-2.0).
